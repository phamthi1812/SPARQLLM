# Advanced Multi-CSV Questions

These questions test more complex reasoning and SPARQL generation capabilities.

## Category 1: Self-Joins (Manager-Employee Relationships)

### Q1.1: Manager Details
**Question:** "Show each department with its manager's name, salary, and years of experience"
**Challenge:** Need to join departments.manager_id with employees.employee_id (self-join concept)
**Expected:** Join departments and employees where dept.manager_id = emp.employee_id

### Q1.2: Manager vs Team Comparison
**Question:** "For each department, show the manager's salary and the average salary of their team members"
**Challenge:** Need two different joins - one for manager, one for team aggregation
**Expected:** Complex query with subquery or multiple aggregations

### Q1.3: Non-Manager Employees
**Question:** "Find all employees who are NOT department managers"
**Challenge:** Need to use NOT EXISTS or left join with filter
**Expected:** Anti-join pattern

---

## Category 2: Nested Aggregations

### Q2.1: Above Average Salaries
**Question:** "Find employees whose salary is above the average salary in their department"
**Challenge:** Need subquery or window function to compare individual to department average
**Expected:** Nested query with GROUP BY in subquery

### Q2.2: Departments Above Company Average
**Question:** "Which departments have an average salary higher than the overall company average?"
**Challenge:** Need to calculate company average, then compare to department averages
**Expected:** Subquery with HAVING clause

### Q2.3: Top Performer Per Department
**Question:** "Show the highest-rated employee in each department"
**Challenge:** Need to partition by department and find max rating
**Expected:** Window function or subquery with MAX

---

## Category 3: Complex Multi-Condition Queries

### Q3.1: Budget Efficiency Analysis
**Question:** "Find departments where the total payroll is less than 50% of their budget"
**Challenge:** Need to aggregate salaries, compare to budget with calculation
**Expected:** GROUP BY with SUM, HAVING clause with arithmetic

### Q3.2: Experience vs Performance
**Question:** "Find employees with more than 10 years experience but performance rating below 4.0"
**Challenge:** Multiple filters across different columns
**Expected:** Multiple FILTER conditions

### Q3.3: Multi-Location Department Analysis
**Question:** "For departments located in New York, find employees working remotely or in different cities"
**Challenge:** Join + filter on department location + filter on employee location mismatch
**Expected:** Complex WHERE with location comparison

---

## Category 4: Ranking and Comparison

### Q4.1: Salary Percentile
**Question:** "Find the top 20% earners in each department"
**Challenge:** Need ranking within groups
**Expected:** Window function or complex subquery

### Q4.2: Department Size Comparison
**Question:** "List departments with more employees than the Sales department"
**Challenge:** Need to count employees per dept, then compare to Sales count
**Expected:** Subquery to get Sales count, then filter

### Q4.3: Budget vs Headcount Ratio
**Question:** "Show departments ranked by budget per employee (highest to lowest)"
**Challenge:** Calculate ratio of budget to employee count
**Expected:** Aggregation with calculated field, ORDER BY

---

## Category 5: Multiple Data Integration Patterns

### Q5.1: Three-Way Join
**Question:** "Show employees, their managers, and both of their departments"
**Challenge:** Need to join employees twice (once for employee, once for manager)
**Expected:** Multiple joins with different aliases

### Q5.2: Cross-Department Analysis
**Question:** "Find employees who earn more than the average salary of a different department (compare each employee to all other departments)"
**Challenge:** Multiple comparisons across departments
**Expected:** Complex join with aggregation

### Q5.3: Hire Date Patterns
**Question:** "Which department has hired the most employees in the last 5 years?"
**Challenge:** Date filtering + aggregation + ordering
**Expected:** Date arithmetic, GROUP BY, COUNT, ORDER BY

---

## Category 6: String Operations and Pattern Matching

### Q6.1: Position Level Analysis
**Question:** "Find all 'Senior' and 'VP' level employees grouped by department"
**Challenge:** String pattern matching (contains "Senior" or "VP")
**Expected:** FILTER with REGEX or CONTAINS

### Q6.2: Email Domain Analysis
**Question:** "List employees whose email domain doesn't match their location's expected domain"
**Challenge:** String extraction from email + comparison logic
**Expected:** REGEX or string functions

### Q6.3: Name Pattern Search
**Question:** "Find employees whose names start with the same letter as their department name"
**Challenge:** String comparison across joined tables
**Expected:** SUBSTR or STRSTARTS functions

---

## Category 7: Advanced Aggregations

### Q7.1: Multi-Level Aggregation
**Question:** "What percentage of the total company payroll does each department represent?"
**Challenge:** Need total payroll AND department payroll, then calculate percentage
**Expected:** Nested aggregation or UNION with totals

### Q7.2: Variance Analysis
**Question:** "Which department has the highest salary variance (difference between highest and lowest paid employees)?"
**Challenge:** Calculate MAX - MIN per department
**Expected:** GROUP BY with MAX and MIN in same query

### Q7.3: Multiple Metrics Dashboard
**Question:** "For each department, show: employee count, total payroll, average salary, average years of experience, and average performance rating"
**Challenge:** Multiple aggregation functions in single query
**Expected:** GROUP BY with multiple aggregate functions

---

## Category 8: Conditional Logic

### Q8.1: Tiered Performance Bonus
**Question:** "Categorize employees as 'Exceeds' (rating > 4.5), 'Meets' (rating 3.5-4.5), or 'Below' (rating < 3.5) by department"
**Challenge:** Need CASE statement or bindings with conditions
**Expected:** BIND with IF or CASE-like logic

### Q8.2: Budget Alert Status
**Question:** "Mark departments as 'Over Budget' if payroll exceeds 80% of budget, 'At Risk' if 60-80%, 'Healthy' if below 60%"
**Challenge:** Complex conditional logic with thresholds
**Expected:** Multiple BIND with conditional expressions

---

## Category 9: Set Operations

### Q9.1: Exclusive Locations
**Question:** "Find locations that have employees but NO department headquarters"
**Challenge:** Set difference operation
**Expected:** MINUS or NOT EXISTS pattern

### Q9.2: Full Location Coverage
**Question:** "Find locations that have BOTH employees AND department headquarters"
**Challenge:** Set intersection
**Expected:** Join or EXISTS for both conditions

### Q9.3: Location Gap Analysis
**Question:** "List all combinations of employee locations and department locations that don't currently exist in the data"
**Challenge:** Need cartesian product minus existing combinations
**Expected:** Complex set operation

---

## Category 10: Performance Optimization Challenges

### Q10.1: Efficient Large Query
**Question:** "Find high performers in high-budget departments, but only load the minimum necessary data"
**Challenge:** Test LLM's ability to write efficient queries
**Expected:** Proper FILTER placement, avoid unnecessary data loading

### Q10.2: Minimal Join Query
**Question:** "List employee names and department names without loading unnecessary columns"
**Challenge:** Select only needed columns from CSVs
**Expected:** Selective property access in GRAPH patterns

---

## Success Criteria for Advanced Questions

✅ **Self-Joins**: Can the LLM join same CSV with different aliases?
✅ **Subqueries**: Can it generate nested SELECT statements?
✅ **Complex Aggregations**: Multiple aggregate functions with GROUP BY and HAVING?
✅ **String Operations**: REGEX, CONTAINS, SUBSTR functions?
✅ **Date Operations**: Date filtering and calculations?
✅ **Conditional Logic**: BIND with IF or CASE-like constructs?
✅ **Set Operations**: UNION, MINUS, NOT EXISTS patterns?
✅ **Anti-Joins**: Finding records that DON'T match?
✅ **Window Functions**: Ranking within groups (if SPARQL supports)?
✅ **Query Optimization**: Efficient query structure?

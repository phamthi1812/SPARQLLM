# Sample Questions for Multi-CSV Demo

## Category 1: Single Source Queries (No JOIN needed)

### Q1.1: Simple employee query
What are the top 5 highest paid employees?
Expected: Query employees.csv only, ORDER BY salary DESC, LIMIT 5

### Q1.2: Simple department query
Which department has the largest budget?
Expected: Query departments.csv only, ORDER BY budget DESC, LIMIT 1

### Q1.3: Employee filtering
Show me all employees with performance rating above 4.5
Expected: Query employees.csv only, FILTER performance_rating > 4.5

---

## Category 2: JOIN Queries (Multiple Sources)

### Q2.1: Basic join
Show employees in the Engineering department with their salaries
Expected: JOIN employees + departments WHERE department_name = "Engineering"

### Q2.2: Join with filtering
Find employees with salary over 100000 in departments located in San Francisco
Expected: JOIN employees + departments, FILTER salary and location

### Q2.3: Manager lookup (self-join pattern)
Show me all department managers with their employee details
Expected: JOIN departments.manager_id = employees.employee_id

---

## Category 3: Aggregation with JOIN

### Q3.1: Group by department
What is the average salary in each department?
Expected: JOIN + GROUP BY department_name + AVG(salary)

### Q3.2: Count by department
How many employees does each department have?
Expected: JOIN + GROUP BY department_name + COUNT(*)

### Q3.3: Complex aggregation
Which departments have average salary above 90000 and more than 3 employees?
Expected: JOIN + GROUP BY + HAVING AVG(salary) > 90000 AND COUNT > 3

---

## Category 4: UNION Queries (Combining Sources)

### Q4.1: Union distinct values
What are all the unique office locations across the company?
Expected: UNION of locations from employees + departments

### Q4.2: Union with labels
List all locations and indicate if they're from employees or departments
Expected: UNION with source label

---

## Category 5: Multi-Condition Cross-Source

### Q5.1: Complex filtering
Find high performers in well-funded departments
Expected: JOIN with FILTER on performance_rating (employees) AND budget (departments)

### Q5.2: Location-based join
Show San Francisco employees with their department budgets
Expected: JOIN + FILTER employees.location = "San Francisco", include budget from departments

---

## Category 6: Data Integration (LLM Knowledge Test)

### Q6.1: Attribute source awareness
Show employee names, their department names, and department budgets
Expected: LLM must know names from employees, department_name from departments, budget from departments

### Q6.2: Smart filtering
Find employees in high-budget departments (over 1 million)
Expected: LLM must know budget is in departments.csv, need JOIN

### Q6.3: Mixed attributes
List employees with their positions, salaries, and the floor their department is on
Expected: position + salary from employees, floor from departments via JOIN

---
